01 — About

I want to know why it broke.

I graduated with a Computer Science degree from Commonwealth University–Mansfield. The part of the work I actually enjoy is the troubleshooting — reproducing the problem, reading the whole error instead of skimming it, and changing one thing at a time until something gives.

My programming is mostly coursework and personal projects: Python, Java, C++, C#, and a good amount of SQL. The database work is what stuck. Designing a schema that holds up and writing a query that answers a real question is the part I keep going back to — it's what both projects below are built around.

I've also been working customer-facing jobs since 2017 — retail, food service, moving, sales. None of it was software. But it's where I learned to stay steady when someone's frustrated and explain a fix in plain language, and for help desk work that turns out to count for a lot.

Right now I'm looking for IT support work and building more projects on the side.
