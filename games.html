Arcade.

Seven games, same rules as the one on the portfolio: written from scratch, no libraries, no build step. Every score is kept in your own browser and nowhere else.

01 / Snake

Don't bite yourself.

Grid movement on a fixed tick, with the tick shortening as you grow. Food never spawns on top of the snake, and reversing directly into your own neck is rejected rather than being an instant loss.

02 / Memory

Eight pairs. Fewest moves wins.

The deck is shuffled with a Fisher-Yates pass, so every layout is equally likely. Input locks while a non-matching pair is face up, which stops you racking up free information by spam-clicking.

04 / Tetris

Seven pieces, one bag at a time.

Pieces come from a shuffled seven-bag, so you never get four S-pieces in a row. Rotation tries a set of wall kicks before giving up, there's a ghost piece showing the landing spot, and gravity tightens every ten lines.

05 / Minesweeper

Your first click is always safe.

Mines are placed after you click, skipping the cell you picked and everything around it, so you never lose on move one. Empty regions open with an iterative flood fill. Right-click to flag, or press and hold on a touchscreen.

06 / Flap

One button. Endless regret.

Constant gravity, a fixed upward impulse per tap, and gaps that narrow slightly as you score. The hitbox is a circle slightly smaller than the sprite, which is the difference between hard and unfair.

07 / Coil

Grow long. Don't touch anyone.

A slither.io-style arena. Your worm follows the cursor with a capped turn rate, so you can't pivot on the spot. Run your head into a rival and you're done. Cut one off and it breaks apart into pellets you can eat.
