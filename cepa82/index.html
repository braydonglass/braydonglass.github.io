Grown Without Compromise

Nothing about it is incidental.

Concentric Architecture

Each layer is a complete modified leaf, laid down in strict sequence from the growth point outward. Nothing is added later.

The Golden Tunic

Three to four outermost scales desiccate into a translucent papery shell, sealing the bulb against moisture loss for months without intervention.

Lachrymatory Precision

Alliinase and the sulfoxide substrates are held in separate compartments until the moment the tissue is breached. The reaction is instantaneous and by design.

The Root Plate

A compressed basal stem, no more than a few millimetres deep, from which every layer above it originates.

Maillard Response

Held at low heat, the reducing sugars and free amino acids undergo a slow browning that develops over forty minutes and cannot be rushed.

Cellar Reserve

Cured in shade at 4°C and 65% relative humidity, the bulb enters dormancy and holds condition through the winter.
